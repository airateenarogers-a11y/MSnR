## 2. Core Candlestick & Market Structure Mechanics

### 2.1 OHLC Anatomy & Price Story Reading (Open, High, Low, Close)
Every candlestick tells a story of the battle between buyers and sellers during a specific timeframe:
- **Open (O)**: Starting price of the timeframe session.
- **High (H)**: Maximum push by buyers (upper wick tip).
- **Low (L)**: Maximum push by sellers (lower wick tip).
- **Close (C)**: Final settlement price.

```
       BULLISH CANDLE                        BEARISH CANDLE
       HIGH                                  HIGH
         |                                     |
    +----+----+  <-- CLOSE                +----+----+  <-- OPEN
    |         |                           |         |
    |  BODY   |                           |  BODY   |
    |         |                           |         |
    +----+----+  <-- OPEN                 +----+----+  <-- CLOSE
         |                                     |
        LOW                                   LOW
```

**Reading the Price Story**:
- A long upper wick indicates seller rejection (supply entering).
- A long lower wick indicates buyer rejection (demand entering).
- A strong, full-bodied candle closing near its extreme shows overwhelming directional dominance.

### 2.2 Candle Range Theory (CRT) Fundamentals
Candle Range Theory (CRT) evaluates price movement by analyzing whether price remains inside or expands outside a prior candle's High and Low range:
- **Inside Bar (Accumulation)**: Price stays within the prior candle's High-Low range.
- **Sweep / Manipulation**: Price wicks out of the High or Low of the CRT range to grab liquidity.
- **Expansion / Distribution**: Price breaks and closes outside the CRT range, initiating a strong trend.

*Note*: As White SNT emphasizes, trading SNR levels located *inside* an unexpanded CRT range yields poor risk-reward. Wait for the CRT boundary sweep or breakout first!

### 2.3 Open Close Level (OCL): The Hidden Moving Average & Hidden Base
White SNT introduces the **Open Close Level (OCL)** as one of the most powerful institutional concepts:
- **What is OCL?**: An OCL is a horizontal line drawn at the exact price point where two adjacent candle bodies meet (where Candle 1 Closes and Candle 2 Opens).
- **Institutional Meaning**: On higher timeframes (HTF like Daily or 4H), an OCL looks like a simple flat meeting point between candle bodies. However, when you zoom down to lower timeframes (15M, 5M, 1M), that exact price line reveals a **fresh Order Block / Hidden Supply & Demand Base**.
- **OCL Properties**:
  - Acts like a dynamic moving average with fixed structural support/resistance.
  - Institutional orders sit stacked at OCLs. When price retests an HTF OCL, it reacts sharply.

```
  HTF View (Daily / 4H):                   LTF View (15M / 5M):
  +-------+                                +-------+
  |  RED  |                                |  OB   | <-- Order Block Base
  | CANDLE|                                +-------+
  +=======+  <-- OCL LINE (Close=Open) ========> Exact OCL Reaction Zone
  | GREEN |                                +-------+
  | CANDLE|                                | RALLY |
  +-------+                                +-------+
```

### 2.4 Structural Swings: Swing Highs, Swing Lows, BOS, and Market Structure Shift (MSS)
Market structure dictates whether a market is trending or reversing:
- **Uptrend**: Series of Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL).
- **Downtrend**: Series of Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL).
- **Break of Structure (BOS)**: A candle body close beyond a previous swing in the direction of the current trend (confirming trend continuation).
- **Market Structure Shift (MSS) / Choch**: A candle body close beyond the last key swing low in an uptrend (or key swing high in a downtrend), signaling structural reversal.

## 3. Malaysian Support & Resistance (MSNR) Foundations

### 3.1 Resistance: The A-Shape Level (Bullish Candle followed by Bearish Candle)
An **A-Shape Level** represents Resistance (Supply). It is formed when a bullish candle is followed immediately by a bearish candle, forming a sharp peak (resembling the letter "A").

```
  A-SHAPE RESISTANCE (Peak)
       [+]
      /     GREEN   RED      <-- Horizontal line drawn at the peak body/wick high
  CANDLE CANDLE
```

### 3.2 Support: The V-Shape Level (Bearish Candle followed by Bullish Candle)
A **V-Shape Level** represents Support (Demand). It is formed when a bearish candle is followed immediately by a bullish candle, forming a sharp trough (resembling the letter "V").

```
  V-SHAPE SUPPORT (Trough)
  RED   GREEN
  CANDLE CANDLE
      \   /        <-- Horizontal line drawn at the trough body/wick low
       [-]
```

### 3.3 GAP Levels: Momentum Price Gaps (Bullish vs. Bearish)
A **GAP Level** occurs when two consecutive candles move aggressively in the same direction, leaving a structural gap or hidden order area:
- **Bullish GAP**: Formed by two consecutive green candles.
- **Bearish GAP**: Formed by two consecutive red candles.
- **How GAP Levels Act**:
  - A **Bullish GAP** acts as **Support** when price drops back to retest it.
  - A **Bearish GAP** acts as **Resistance** when price rallies back to retest it.

```
  BULLISH GAP SUPPORT                BEARISH GAP RESISTANCE
     +-------+                          +-------+
     | GREEN |                          |  RED  |
     +-------+                          +-------+
     | GREEN | <-- GAP Line             |  RED  | <-- GAP Line
     +-------+                          +-------+
```

### 3.4 Broken Levels: Support Becomes Resistance (SBR) & Resistance Becomes Support (RBS)
When a market structure breaks, level roles flip cleanly:
- **SBR (Support Becomes Resistance)**: A V-shape support or Bullish GAP is broken downward by a candle **body close**. Once broken, this level flips to act as **Resistance** for future rallies.
- **RBS (Resistance Becomes Support)**: An A-shape resistance or Bearish GAP is broken upward by a candle **body close**. Once broken, this level flips to act as **Support** for future dips.

### 3.5 Freshness Rules: Never Touched, Touched by Wick (Unfresh), Broken by Body (Fresh Re-activation)
Level state management is the single most critical filter in MSNR:

1. **Fresh Level (100% Active)**:
   - The level has **never been touched** by any candle wick since its creation.
   - *Power*: Highest probability level for a trade reaction.
2. **Unfresh Level (Weakened / Expired)**:
   - The level has been **poked or touched by a candle wick** without a body close beyond it.
   - *Power*: Exhausted. Do NOT take trades directly from unfresh levels unless repaired by a Confirmation Candle (CC) or HTF storyline alignment.
3. **Fresh Re-activation via Body Close**:
   - When a level is **broken cleanly by a candle body close**, it transforms into an SBR or RBS level, turning **100% FRESH AGAIN** as a new broken level!

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            LEVEL FRESHNESS STATE MATRIX                           |
+---------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------+
| Event               | Level State                     | Actionable Trade Status   |
+---------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------+
| Newly Created       | 100% FRESH                      | READY FOR FIRST TOUCH     |
| Touched by Wick     | UNFRESH (Mitigated)             | DO NOT TRADE (Avoid)      |
| Repaired by CC      | FRESH REPAIRED                  | VALID FOR TRADE           |
| Broken by Body      | FRESH SBR / RBS (Role Flip)     | READY FOR FIRST RETEST    |
+---------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------+
```

### 3.6 The Confirmation Candle (CC) Rule: 3rd-Candle Level Repair Mechanism
Pitchou.FX outlines a rare, vital exception for unfresh levels called the **Confirmation Candle (CC) Rule**:
- **Definition**: The **Confirmation Candle (CC)** is the 3rd candle that forms immediately after an A-shape or V-shape level is created.
- **Condition**:
  - On a **V-shape Support**, the 3rd candle MUST be a **Green (Bullish) Candle**.
  - On an **A-shape Resistance**, the 3rd candle MUST be a **Red (Bearish) Candle**.
- **Effect**: If an unfresh level possesses a valid CC, it is **repaired and treated as a valid Fresh Level** again!

In Malaysian SNR, trendlines are drawn strictly through structural peaks and troughs:
- **Uptrend Line (Support TL)**: Drawn by connecting two or more **V-Shape troughs** sloping upward.
- **Downtrend Line (Resistance TL)**: Drawn by connecting two or more **A-Shape peaks** sloping downward.

```
  UPTREND LINE (Support TL)              DOWNTREND LINE (Resistance TL)
             / Peak 2                               Peak 1             /                                               \ Peak 2
     V-Shape 2                                                   /                                                         V-Shape 1
  V-Shape 1
```

### 4.2 The 8 Trendline Types & The Strict 5-Touch Expiration Rule
Trendlines track structural liquidity. Pitchou.FX defines 8 distinct trendline variations based on timeframe and slope combinations.

**The Strict 5-Touch Limit Rule**:
- Every trendline has a maximum lifespan of **5 touches**.
- **Touch 1 & 2**: Create and establish the trendline.
- **Touch 3**: **THE GOLDEN TOUCH** — The highest probability entry touch!
- **Touch 4**: Tradeable with strict lower-timeframe confirmation badges.
- **Touch 5**: Final touch.
- **After Touch 5**: The trendline is considered **EXPIRED & EXHAUSTED**. Expect a breakout on or after the 5th touch!

### 4.3 The 3rd Touch Rule: Maximum Probability Entry
The 3rd touch on a trendline represents the sweet spot where trend direction is confirmed but liquidity has not been depleted. When a 3rd touch aligns with a **fresh SNR level**, it creates a 10/10 confluence trade setup!

### 4.4 White SNT's Trendline Key (TL Key) & Inducement (IDM) Liquidity Sweeps

## 5. Quasimodo (QM) Pattern Breakdown & Validation

### 5.1 Anatomy of Bearish QM (High, Low, Higher High, Lower Low)
A **Bearish QM Pattern** signals a major reversal from a bullish trend to a bearish trend.

**Key Levels of Bearish QM**:
1. **High (H)**: The initial peak (A-shape).
2. **Low (L)**: The first trough (V-shape) following the High.
3. **Higher High (HH)**: The second peak, rising above the High (A-shape). Creates a buyer trap!
4. **Lower Low (LL)**: The second trough, crashing cleanly below the first Low. **This body breakout confirms the Bearish QM Pattern!**

```
  BEARISH QM PATTERN ANATOMY
            HH (Peak 2)
           /      H (1) /         /\  /      \  ENTRY ZONE (Left Shoulder / SBR / Apex)
    /  \/        \   |
   /   L (1)      \--+--> Retest Entry
  /                                    LL (Breaks below L - Confirms QM!)
```

### 5.2 Anatomy of Bullish QM (Low, High, Lower Low, Higher High)
A **Bullish QM Pattern** signals a major reversal from a bearish trend to a bullish trend.

**Key Levels of Bullish QM**:
1. **Low (L)**: The initial trough (V-shape).
2. **High (H)**: The first peak (A-shape) following the Low.
3. **Lower Low (LL)**: The second trough, dipping below the Low (V-shape). Creates a seller trap!
4. **Higher High (HH)**: The second peak, surging cleanly above the first High. **This body breakout confirms the Bullish QM Pattern!**

```
  BULLISH QM PATTERN ANATOMY
                    HH (Breaks above H - Confirms QM!)
                   /
     H (1)        /  ENTRY ZONE (Left Shoulder / RBS / Apex)
      /\         /--+--> Retest Entry
     /  \       /
    /    \     /
   /      \   /
  L (1)    \ /
            LL (Trough 2)
```

### 5.3 Valid Breakout vs. Fakeout (Candle Body Close vs. Wick Sweep)
The golden rule of Malaysian SNR breakout validation:
- **Valid Breakout**: A candle **BODY MUST CLOSE** beyond the key structural level (beyond Low for Bearish QM, beyond High for Bullish QM).
- **Fakeout / Invalid Breakout**: If a candle pokes past the level with a wick but the **body closes inside/behind the level**, it is a **FAKEOUT / LIQUIDITY SWEEP**. Do NOT treat it as a structural breakout!

### 5.4 External Breakout vs. Internal Breakout Rules
- **External Breakout (VALID)**: The breakout occurs on the level created **BEFORE** price touches the fresh SNR level. This breaks main market structure.
- **Internal Breakout (INVALID)**: The breakout occurs on a level created **AFTER** price touches the fresh SNR level. Internal breakouts take place inside structure and do not confirm a trend shift.

### 5.5 Post-Touch Breakout Differences: SNR Levels vs. Trendlines
Pitchou.FX identifies a critical structural difference:
- **For SNR Levels**: The breakout MUST happen **after** the last touch on the SNR level. If a QM pattern forms before or during the touch without a post-touch breakout, it is invalid.
- **For Trendlines**: A QM pattern formed **after or during** a trendline touch remains **FULLY VALID**, because trendlines adjust with market momentum!

### 5.6 SMC Cross-Over: QM Apex = Turtle Soup Sweep; Left Shoulder = Order Block / SBR / RBS
Connecting Pitchou.FX and White SNT:
- **QM Apex**: Equivalent to an ICT **Turtle Soup / Liquidity Sweep** of previous highs/lows.
- **QM Left Shoulder (LS)**: Equivalent to an **Order Block / Breaker Block / HTF OCL**. Price returns to the Left Shoulder to mitigate orders before launching the trend.

### 6.5 The 3 Parts of a Storyline: Part 1 (Initiation / QM Reversal), Part 2 (Middle Expansion), Part 3 (Ending Exhaustion)

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

1. **Part 1 (Start / Initiation)**:

   - Characterized by the initial **QM Pattern Formation** (Apex, Left Shoulder, SBR/RBS).

3. **Part 3 (End / Target Exhaustion)**:
   - Price approaches the destination HTF SNR level or trendline.
   - Trend momentum slows down; prepare to take profits before Part 1 of the opposite storyline begins.

- **Bearish ERS**: A mini A-shape forming right before the bearish breakout.
- **Trading ERS**: ERS levels act as high-precision early entry checkpoints when aligned with a short-term trendline!
