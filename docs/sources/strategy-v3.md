## 2. CANDLESTICK ANATOMY & PRICE STORY MECHANICS

### 2.1 The OHLC Breakdown (Open, High, Low, Close)
Every candlestick represents four fundamental data points that reveal the underlying battle between buyers (bulls) and sellers (bears):
* **Open (O):** The initial transaction price when the timeframe period opens.
* **High (H):** The highest price point reached during the period, representing the maximum reach of bullish expansion.
* **Low (L):** The lowest price point reached during the period, representing the maximum reach of bearish pressure.
* **Close (C):** The final transaction price when the period closes, determining candle color and structural body boundaries.

```
       BULLISH CANDLE                        BEARISH CANDLE
       High ----------                      High ----------
         |                                    |
       Close =========                      Open  =========
         |           |                        |           |
         |   Body    |                        |   Body    |
         |           |                        |           |
       Open  =========                      Close =========
         |                                    |
       Low  ----------                      Low  ----------
```

### 2.2 Candle Range Theory (CRT) & Institutional Narrative
**Candle Range Theory (CRT)** states that an individual higher-timeframe (HTF) candlestick (such as a Daily, Weekly, or Monthly candle) represents an entire micro-trend cycle on lower timeframes:
* The **Wick** represents liquidity sweeps and rejection tail-ends (manipulation/reversal phases on LTF).
* The **Body** represents high-volume directional momentum (distribution/expansion phases on LTF).
* **Price Story:** By evaluating where the candle opens relative to its High/Low extremes and where it closes, traders discern whether smart money accumulated or distributed liquidity during that timeframe.

### 2.3 Open Close Levels (OCL) — The Hidden Order Blocks
An **Open Close Level (OCL)** is defined by White SNT as the horizontal price line where two adjacent candles meet at their Open and Close boundaries (for example, where a bullish candle closes and the next candle opens, or where a bearish candle closes and a bullish candle engulfs it).

#### **Key OCL Properties:**
1. **HTF Order Block Equivalence:** On a Higher Timeframe (e.g., 1H or 4H), an OCL appears as a simple boundary line. On Lower Timeframes (M15, M5, M1), that exact price level contains a refined **Order Block / Base Pattern**.
2. **Fixed Period Moving Average Effect:** Mechanically, an OCL acts as a fixed institutional benchmark where market makers rebalance unfilled buy/sell orders.
3. **P-Type & D-P Patterns:** OCLs form key pivot benchmarks where price retests prior institutional order injections before surging in the trend direction.

## 3. MALAYSIAN SUPPORT & RESISTANCE (MSNR) FOUNDATIONS

In Malaysian SNR, market structure is defined strictly using **three specific level types**. Micro-swings and random candles are filtered out by rigid structural definitions.

### 3.1 A-Shape Level (Resistance)
* **Formation Rule:** Formed when a **bullish candle** is immediately followed by a **bearish candle**, creating a sharp peak.
* **Market Function:** Acts as a **Resistance Ceiling**. It halts upward momentum and acts as a price level where selling interest overcomes buying pressure.

```
      A-SHAPE (RESISTANCE)
          Peak (High)
           /\  <--- Resistance Line
          /       Bullish  Bearish
     Candle   Candle
```

### 3.2 V-Shape Level (Support)
* **Formation Rule:** Formed when a **bearish candle** is immediately followed by a **bullish candle**, creating a sharp trough.
* **Market Function:** Acts as a **Support Floor**. It halts downward momentum and acts as a price level where buying interest overcomes selling pressure.

```
      V-SHAPE (SUPPORT)
     Bearish  Bullish
     Candle   Candle
          \  /
           \/  <--- Support Line
         Trough (Low)
```

### 3.3 GAP Levels (Bullish & Bearish GAPs)
* **Formation Rule:** Formed between two consecutive candles moving aggressively in the **same direction** without wick/body overlap between the first candle's body/wick and the second candle's body/wick, leaving an open horizontal price gap.
* **Bullish GAP Level:** Formed during an aggressive upward surge. The open gap zone acts as a future **Support Level**.
* **Bearish GAP Level:** Formed during an aggressive downward drop. The open gap zone acts as a future **Resistance Level**.

```
    BULLISH GAP (SUPPORT)                 BEARISH GAP (RESISTANCE)
       Candle 2 [Bull]                       Candle 1 [Bear]
       ==============                        ==============
       |            |                        |            |
       ==============                        ==============
      -------------- <--- GAP ZONE --->     --------------
       ==============                        ==============
       |            |                        |            |
       ==============                        ==============
       Candle 1 [Bull]                       Candle 2 [Bear]
```

### 3.4 Fresh vs. Unfresh Level Mitigation Rules
In MSNR, level freshness dictates trade probability:

* **Fresh Level:**
  1. An SNR level that has **never been touched by any subsequent candle wick**.
  2. OR an SNR level that was **broken completely by a candle body close** (a clean body close breaks structure and creates/refreshes a new structural level, e.g., SBR or RBS).
* **Unfresh Level:**
  * An SNR level that has already been **touched or tested by a candle wick**. Once a wick touches the level, liquidity at that level is mitigated/exhausted, reducing probability for future direct bounces.

```
       [FRESH LEVEL]                           [UNFRESH LEVEL]
   Never touched OR broken                  Touched by candle wick
    cleanly by body close.                  (liquidity mitigated).
```

### 3.5 The Confirmation Candle (CC) 3rd-Candle Repair Rule
When an SNR level has been touched by a wick (making it unfresh), Pitchou.FX provides a rare structural rule that restores its validity: **The Confirmation Candle (CC) Rule**.

* **Rule:** A level with a wick touch remains **valid and usable** if the **3rd candle immediately following the creation of the level** closes in the direction of the level:
  * **CC for Support Levels:** The 3rd candle must close as a **GREEN (Bullish) Candle**.
  * **CC for Resistance Levels:** The 3rd candle must close as a **RED (Bearish) Candle**.
* **Usage Limit:** An SNR level can be traded up to **two times maximum**: First on a fresh touch with CC confirmation, second on a touch-and-breakout setup. Beyond two uses, probability drops significantly.

## 4. TRENDLINES & LIQUIDITY KEYS

### 4.1 All 8 Types of Trendlines (Pitchou.FX)
Trendlines connect consecutive A-shape peaks or V-shape troughs to track market slope and dynamic liquidity:
1. **Bullish Reversal Trendline:** Connects declining peaks prior to a bullish reversal.
2. **Bearish Reversal Trendline:** Connects ascending troughs prior to a bearish reversal.
3. **Bullish QM Trendline:** Drawn through a Quasimodo structure confirming a bullish transition.
4. **Bearish QM Trendline:** Drawn through a Quasimodo structure confirming a bearish transition.
5. **Bullish Outer Trendline:** Drawn across major external low points of a market expansion.
6. **Bearish Outer Trendline:** Drawn across major external high points of a market expansion.
7. **Bullish Break & Retest Trendline:** Resistance trendline broken upward and retested as support.
8. **Bearish Break & Retest Trendline:** Support trendline broken downward and retested as resistance.

### 4.2 The 5-Touch Limit & 3rd-Touch Golden Entry Rule
* **Maximum Touches:** A trendline remains valid for up to **5 touches**. Beyond 5 touches, the trendline is considered structurally exhausted.
* **The 3rd Touch Rule:** Statistically, the **3rd touch** of a trendline delivers the highest-probability bounce or breakout trade setup.

### 4.3 Trendline Key (TL Key) & Inducement (IDM) Sweeps (White SNT)
White SNT expands trendline mechanics by identifying trendlines as retail liquidity pools:
* **Trendline Liquidity (TL Liquidity):** Retail traders place stop losses directly behind trendlines.
* **Inducement (IDM):** Smart money pushes price to pierce a trendline (sweeping retail stop losses) before immediately reversing back into the true trend direction.
* **Trendline Key (TL Key):** The exact price level where an IDM sweep intersects an HTF SNR or Open Close Level (OCL), creating a high-confluence entry zone.

## 5. BREAKOUT MECHANICS & QUASIMODO (QM) PATTERN

### 5.1 Valid Breakout Criteria (Candle Body Close vs. Wick Fakeout)
In Malaysian SNR, the distinction between a true breakout and a fakeout is absolute:
* **Valid Breakout:** Confirmed **ONLY** when a candle **BODY CLOSES CLEARLY BEYOND** the SNR level or trendline.
* **False Breakout (Fakeout / Wick Sweep):** Occurs when a candle wick pierces beyond the level but its **body closes inside** the level. This is a liquidity sweep, not a breakout.

```
      VALID BREAKOUT (BODY CLOSE)              FAKEOUT (WICK SWEEP)
      ---------------------------              ---------------------------
            |                                         |
          ======  <-- Body closes                   ----- <-- Wick pierces
          |    |      beyond line.                  |   |
          ======                                    ===== <-- Body closes
            |                                       |   |     inside line.
```

### 5.2 Anatomy of the Quasimodo Pattern (High, Low, Higher High, Lower Low)
A breakout is structurally valid under MSNR **only if it forms a valid Quasimodo (QM) Pattern**:

```
       BEARISH QM PATTERN                      BULLISH QM PATTERN
             HH (Higher High)                      HH (Higher High)
            /  \                                  /        H (High)  \                           H (High)       /           \                         /               /             LL (Lower Low)          /             LL (Lower Low)
   L (Low)                               L (Low)
```

#### **Structural Criteria:**
* **Bearish QM Pattern:**
  1. **High (H):** Initial peak.
  2. **Low (L):** Initial trough.
  3. **Higher High (HH):** Price expands above High (H), sweeping liquidity.
  4. **Lower Low (LL):** Price drops aggressively below Low (L), breaking structure with a candle body close to confirm a valid bearish breakout.
* **Bullish QM Pattern:**
  1. **Low (L):** Initial trough.
  2. **High (H):** Initial peak.
  3. **Lower Low (LL):** Price drops below Low (L), sweeping liquidity.
  4. **Higher High (HH):** Price surges aggressively above High (H), breaking structure with a candle body close to confirm a valid bullish breakout.

### 5.3 Internal vs. External Breakouts
When price approaches a fresh SNR level, breakouts fall into two operational categories:
* **External Bullish Breakout:** Price breaks the last A-shape level formed **BEFORE** touching the fresh SNR support level.
* **Internal Bullish Breakout:** Price breaks the A-shape level formed **AFTER** touching the fresh SNR support level.
* **External Bearish Breakout:** Price breaks the last V-shape level formed **BEFORE** touching the fresh SNR resistance level.
* **Internal Bearish Breakout:** Price breaks the V-shape level formed **AFTER** touching the fresh SNR resistance level.

### 5.4 SNR Level Breakouts vs. Trendline Breakouts (Post-Touch Differences)
* **Breakouts on SNR Levels:** The breakout **MUST occur AFTER the last touch on the SNR level**. If a QM forms before or without respecting the SNR level touch, it is invalid.
* **Breakouts on Trendlines:** A QM pattern formed **after** the touch on a trendline is valid, but **early breakouts on trendlines are also fully valid** because trendlines adjust dynamically with price slope.

## 6. MULTI-TIMEFRAME ALIGNMENT & TIMEFRAME STRUCTURE

### 6.1 Timeframe Rejection Closing Requirements (Monthly to 15M)
Different timeframes require different candle closing signatures to confirm rejections at key levels:

* **Monthly Rejection:** Rejection wick **WITHOUT** requiring a monthly candle body close.
* **Weekly Rejection:** Rejection wick **WITHOUT** requiring a weekly candle body close.
* **Daily Rejection:** Requires **Daily candle body close**.
* **4H Rejection:** Requires **4H candle body close**.
* **1H Rejection:** Requires **4H or 1H candle body close**.
* **30Min Rejection:** Requires **30Min candle body close**.
* **15Min Rejection:** Requires **15Min candle body close**.

### 6.2 Standard MTF Alignment Mapping (HTF SNR -> LTF BO)
To confirm trend shifts and trade entries, follow this multi-timeframe alignment hierarchy:

```

  Daily SNR Level / TL    ---> Rejection ---> 1H QM Breakout
```

### 6.3 Multi-Timeframe Confluence (Monthly + Weekly + Daily Alignment)

### 6.4 Rare Cases in MTF Breakouts
1. **Rare HTF Early Breakouts:** When an HTF rejection creates long wicks, hidden LTF fresh levels inside those wicks can trigger an early breakout on a slightly higher LTF than standard (e.g., Weekly breakout on a Monthly SNR before Daily breakout).

### 8.2 SMT Intermarket Divergence (Gold vs. Silver, EURUSD vs. GBPUSD)
**SMT (Smart Money Tool) Divergence** occurs when two highly correlated assets fail to confirm each other's swing highs or swing lows at key turning points:

#### **Correlated Pairs Matrix:**
* `XAUUSD` (Gold) vs. `XAGUSD` (Silver)
* `EURUSD` vs. `GBPUSD`
* `AUDUSD` vs. `NZDUSD`
* `USDJPY` vs. `USDCHF` (Inverted)

#### **SMT Mechanics:**
* **Bullish SMT:** Asset A makes a Lower Low, but Asset B makes a Higher Low. Asset B shows underlying institutional strength, confirming a high-probability buy setup on both assets.
* **Bearish SMT:** Asset A makes a Higher High, but Asset B makes a Lower High. Asset B shows underlying institutional weakness, confirming a high-probability sell setup.

```
   BULLISH SMT DIVERGENCE:
   Asset A (XAUUSD):  High ----\_____ Lower Low (Sweeps Liquidity)
   Asset B (XAGUSD):  High ----\_____ Higher Low (Holds Strength - BUY CONFIRMED!)
```

* **Q4 (Continuation/Reversal - X):** Trend completion or retracement.

#### **90-Minute Cycle Timetable (UTC):**

* **Cycle 4 (NY PM):** Q1: 18:00-19:30 | Q2: 19:30-21:00 | Q3: 21:00-22:30 | Q4: 22:30-00:00
