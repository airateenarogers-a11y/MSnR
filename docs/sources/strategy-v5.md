## 2. MARKET FOUNDATIONS & SNR LEVEL MECHANICS

### 2.1 Mechanical Level Boundaries
In MSNR 2.0, every support and resistance level has two distinct components:
1. **The Horizontal Level Line (Open/Close Boundary)**: Placed precisely at the **body junction where Candle 1 closes and Candle 2 opens**. This exact horizontal line is used for testing **touches, rejections, and breakouts**.
2. **The Apex Extreme (Wick Tip)**: The highest wick high of an A-Shape or lowest wick low of a V-Shape. Used for **Stop Loss placement** (adding mandatory safety buffers) and lower-timeframe **Apex Refinement**.

```
A-SHAPE RESISTANCE LEVEL                    V-SHAPE SUPPORT LEVEL

       [Apex / Wick High]                         (Candle 1)   (Candle 2)
              /\                                      \           /
             /  \                                      \         /
  (Candle 1)/    \(Candle 2)                            \       /
           /      \                                      \     /
   =======[Body Junction]======= [LEVEL LINE]    =======[Body Junction]======= [LEVEL LINE]
          |        |                                    |       |
          | Red/Grn|                                    |Red/Grn|
                                                           \/
                                                  [Apex / Wick Low]
```

### 2.2 Level Definitions

#### 1. A-Shape Resistance (A-SNR)
* **Formation**: A bullish (or bearish) candle immediately followed by a bearish candle, forming a sharp peak resembling the letter "A".
* **Placement**: Horizontal line drawn at the **close/open body junction** of the two candles.
* **Function**: Acts as a supply ceiling blocking upward movement.

#### 2. V-Shape Support (V-SNR)
* **Formation**: A bearish (or bullish) candle immediately followed by a bullish candle, forming a sharp valley resembling the letter "V".
* **Placement**: Horizontal line drawn at the **close/open body junction** of the two candles.
* **Function**: Acts as a demand floor blocking downward movement.

#### 3. GAP Levels (Overlapping / Structural GAPs)
* **Formation**: Two consecutive candles closing strongly in the **same direction** (two bullish candles for a Bullish GAP, or two bearish candles for a Bearish GAP).
* **Placement**: Horizontal line drawn at the **close/open junction** where Candle 1 closes and Candle 2 opens.
* **Function**: Represents explosive momentum. Bullish GAPs act as strong support; Bearish GAPs act as strong resistance.

```
BULLISH GAP LEVEL (SUPPORT)               BEARISH GAP LEVEL (RESISTANCE)

      [Candle 2 (Bullish)]                      |         |
         |        |                             | Bearish |
         +--------+                             +---------+
   ======| Close 1/Open 2 |====== [GAP LEVEL] =====| Close 1/Open 2 |====== [GAP LEVEL]
         +--------+                             +---------+
         | Bullish|                             | Bearish |
         | Candle1|                             | Candle2 |
```

## 3. CANDLE ANATOMY, OCL & LEVEL FRESHNESS STATES

### 3.1 Candlestick Range Theory (CRT) & OCL
Every candlestick contains four price points (OHLC). In *The Alchemist* framework, the **Open Close Level (OCL)** represents the real structural body of institutional orders:
* **Open Price**: Institutional start of period manipulation.
* **Close Price**: Institutional valuation settlement.
* **High/Low Wicks**: Liquidity sweeps (judas swings) into retail stops.

### 3.2 Level Freshness States & Mitigation Rules

```
+------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Level State      | Definition & Operational Status                                  |
+------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Fresh            | Never touched by a price wick since creation. Highest win rate.  |
| Unfresh          | Touched/poked by a price wick. Mitigation occurs; state dimmed.  |
| Restored (CC)    | Unfresh level validated by a 3rd-candle Confirmation Candle (CC).|
| Broken (RBS/SBR) | Candle body closes past level line. Converts to RBS or SBR.      |
+------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.3 The Confirmation Candle (CC) 3rd-Candle Rule
When an A-Shape or V-Shape level is created, its freshness is strictly determined by the **3rd candle** in the sequence:
1. **Candle 1**: Initial directional candle.
2. **Candle 2**: Opposite candle completing the A/V shape.
3. **Candle 3 (The CC)**: Must close in the **same direction as the newly formed level**:
   * **For V-Support**: Candle 3 must close **Bullish (Green)**.
   * **For A-Resistance**: Candle 3 must close **Bearish (Red)**.
* **Effect**: A valid CC restores an otherwise unfresh/tested level to **full fresh trading authority**.

## 4. TRENDLINE MECHANICS & TOUCH LIMITS

### 4.1 Trendline Construction Rules
Trendlines in MSNR 2.0 must connect exact **A-Shape peaks (Resistance Trendlines)** or **V-Shape valleys (Support Trendlines)**. 

```
               (Touch 1)          (Touch 2)          (Touch 3)          (Touch 4)          (Touch 5)
                 A1                 A2                 A3                 A4                 A5
                 /\                 /\                 /\                 /\                 /                /  \               /  \               /  \               /  \               /    -------------/----\-------------/----\-------------/----\-------------/----\-------------/----\-----> BREAKOUT!
              [Establish]        [Confirm]         [BEST ENTRY]       [VALID ENTRY]      [DO NOT ENTER]
```

### 4.2 The 5-Touch Limit Rule
* **Touches 1 & 2**: Used exclusively to **draw and establish** the trendline angle. No trades allowed.
* **Touches 3 & 4 (Active Entry Touches)**: **Valid trade entry setups**. Touch 3 offers the highest historical win rate (1:3+ RR).
* **Touch 5 (Breakout Limit)**: Trendlines hold up to 5 touches maximum. **Touch 5 is expected to break out**—do NOT take continuation trades on Touch 5.

### 4.3 The 8 Trendline Classification Types
1. **Classic Support Trendline**: Connects 2+ fresh V-shapes pointing upward.
2. **Classic Resistance Trendline**: Connects 2+ fresh A-shapes pointing downward.
3. **Breakout Support Trendline (SBR TL)**: Broken support trendline retested from below as resistance.
4. **Breakout Resistance Trendline (RBS TL)**: Broken resistance trendline retested from above as support.
5. **Advanced Support Trendline**: Connects V-shapes across multi-timeframe storylines.
6. **Advanced Resistance Trendline**: Connects A-shapes across multi-timeframe storylines.
7. **Inducement (IDM) Trendline**: Liquidity trendline engineered by algorithms to trap retail breakout traders.
8. **Trendline Key (TL Key)**: Confluence point where a trendline intersects directly with a fresh horizontal SNR level.

## 5. BREAKOUT VALIDATION & QUASIMODO (QM) PATTERN ANATOMY

### 5.1 Breakout Validation: Body Close vs. Wick Sweep
* **Valid Breakout**: A candle **BODY CLOSES** completely beyond the horizontal SNR level line. Confirms structural breakout and flips level to SBR/RBS.
* **Fakeout / Rejection Sweep**: Only the **WICK** extends past the SNR level line while the body closes inside. Confirms level holds; signals potential reversal.

```
VALID BREAKOUT (Body Close Past Line)          FAKEOUT / REJECTION (Wick Only)

        |  [Green Body Close]                          |  [Wick Sweeps Past]
     +-----+                                           |
-----|-----|---------------- [SNR LINE]       ---------|-------------------- [SNR LINE]
     +-----+                                        +-----+
        |  [Red Candle]                             |     | [Body Closes Inside]
        |                                           +-----+
```

### 5.2 Quasimodo (QM) Pattern Anatomy
The Quasimodo pattern is the ultimate structural reversal pattern in MSNR 2.0.

#### Bullish QM (Buy Setup)
1. Price forms a Low (**L**).
2. Price rallies to form a High (**H**).
3. Price drops below Low to form a Lower Low (**LL**) — sweeping liquidity.
4. Price surges upward and **candle body closes** above High to form a Higher High (**HH**).
5. **Entry Zone**: Pullback to the **Left Shoulder (LS)** level.

```
BULLISH QM ANATOMY                             BEARISH QM ANATOMY

                  HH (Higher High)                         H (High)           HH (Higher High)
                  /\                                       /\                 /                 /  \  [Pullback Entry]                   /  \               /        H (High)  /    \  /                     LS (Left   /    \             /             /\    /      \/ [LS Entry]           Shoulder) /      \           /              /  \  /                               ==========/=======\=========/========\========== [LS Entry]
   ====/====\/                                         /         \       /          \  /
   LS /      L (Low)                                  /           \     /            \/ [Pullback Entry]
  /  /                                               /             \   /             LL (Lower Low)
    /                                               /               \ /
   / LL (Lower Low)                                /                 L (Low)
```

#### Bearish QM (Sell Setup)
1. Price forms a High (**H**).
2. Price drops to form a Low (**L**).
3. Price rallies above High to form a Higher High (**HH**) — sweeping liquidity.
4. Price crashes downward and **candle body closes** below Low to form a Lower Low (**LL**).
5. **Entry Zone**: Pullback to the **Left Shoulder (LS)** level.
